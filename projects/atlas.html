Atlas

A painting as atlas. Each symbol — the plane wing, the Campanile, the piano — is an entrance into a different way that time gets distorted, measured, remembered, or heard. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, press 0 to reset the view.

Symbols shown dim are visible but not yet active — their portals (Plane Wing, Campanile, Piano) are still being built.
