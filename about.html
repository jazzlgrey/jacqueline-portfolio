Jacqueline Song

Welcome to perlgray — a digital gallery by Jacqueline Song.

Raised between Hong Kong and Beijing, I now study Art History and Data Science at UC Berkeley. My work moves between code, painting, and poetry, tracing motion, space, time, and above all, beauty.

I believe beauty is the sincerest way of paying attention.

Outside the gallery, I paint, read, play the piano, cook, and dance Argentine tango.

Jacqueline is available for select projects. For inquiries, please email itsjacquelinehere@gmail.com.
